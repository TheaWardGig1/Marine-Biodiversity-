What To Know

Marine animals contribute to the overall biodiversity of the planet, which is essential for ecosystem resilience and stability

Some marine animals, like beavers or coral reefs, physically alter their environment, creating habitats for other species.

Oceans cover approximately 70% of the planets surface and contain about 97% of Earth's water.

They are home to an estimated 750,000 to 25 million marine species, though only around 243,613 species have been formally classified.

Many marine animals contribute to nutrient cycling, breaking down organic matter and releasing essential nutrients back into the water.

Facts

Coral reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor but support about 25% of all marine species.

Every year, an estimated 8 million tons of plastic enter the oceans, harming countless marine animals.

Whales help fight climate change by storing large amounts of carbon and fertilizing phytoplankton with their waste.

The ocean produces more than half of the world's oxygen and absorbs about 30% of the carbon dioxide produced by humans.

How To Help

Reduce single-use plastics. Choose reusable bags, bottles, and containers to keep trash out of the sea.

Join or organize beach cleanups. Even small local efforts help remove harmful debris from coastlines.

Support marine conservation organizations and choose sustainable seafood to protect ocean ecosystems.

Educate others about marine conservation and support stronger laws that protect ocean habitats from overfishing and pollution.

Raising awareness about climate change, and marine species decline can influence policy and community action.
